# Contributor conduct

Treat people with respect. Discuss code and evidence without personal attacks,
harassment, discrimination or disclosure of another person's private information.
Keep issues and pull requests relevant to the project.

The maintainer may edit or remove abusive content, close disruptive discussions
or restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately through the contact
information at [umbertogiacobbi.biz](https://umbertogiacobbi.biz).
